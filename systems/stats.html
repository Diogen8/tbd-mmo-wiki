Character Stats

The agreed stat vocabulary. Exact values, scaling formulas, damage formulas and balance caps are still open.

On this page
  1. Primary attributes
  2. Derived stats
  3. How modifiers apply
  4. Invariants

Players, NPCs and monsters share the same stat model. What separates them is their definition and the capabilities they have, not a different set of stats.

Primary attributes

AttributeFeeds
STR StrengthPhysical attack, physical critical multiplier
DEX DexterityAccuracy, evasion, attack speed, physical critical chance
CON ConstitutionMax health, health regeneration, part of physical defense
INT IntelligenceMagical attack, magical critical multiplier
WIS WisdomMax mana, mana regeneration, magical defense, cast speed, magical critical chance
CHA CharismaFuture social stats and interactions

Primary attributes are inputs to the derived stats. They are not current resources, and they don’t directly represent an attack result.

Rule: movement speed should not scale strongly or directly from DEX. Otherwise DEX would improve accuracy, evasion, attack frequency, critical chance and mobility all at once, and become a mandatory attribute.

Derived stats

Resources

  • Max health
  • Max mana
  • Health regeneration
  • Mana regeneration

Offense

  • Physical attack
  • Magical attack
  • Accuracy
  • Physical critical chance
  • Physical critical multiplier
  • Magical critical chance
  • Magical critical multiplier
  • Attack speed
  • Cast speed
  • Cooldown reduction

Physical and magical attack are inputs to damage calculation, not final damage values. Attack speed shortens the basic-attack cycle, cast speed shortens ability preparation and casting time, and cooldown reduction shortens the reuse delay after an action completes. These are three separate mechanics.

Defense

  • Physical defense
  • Magical defense
  • Evasion
  • Elemental resistance, per damage type

Elemental resistance is keyed by damage type rather than a fixed field for every possible element. The supported damage types are not decided yet. Resistance may be negative.

Movement

  • Movement speed

Acceleration and deceleration belong to movement itself and are not part of the general stat set.

Current health and current mana are the character’s live resources, not derived stats.

How modifiers apply

Values are resolved in a fixed order:

base values of the character’s definition
+ permanent character modifiers
+ equipment modifiers
+ temporary effects
→ resolved attributes and stats

Each stat follows the same pipeline:

(base + flat modifiers)
× (1 + sum of additive percentages)
× final multipliers
→ clamp for that stat

Every modifier carries a stable source, so everything an item, effect or progression node contributes can be replaced or removed together.
