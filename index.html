01Concept

The top-level vision
Tone & setting

A fragile, contested civilization

Dark, sword-and-magic fantasy, partly in the spirit of Lineage 2: civilization is a fragile, contested achievement rather than a stable backdrop.

Mana is the substance reality itself is made of. A city’s prosperity is upkeep, not a one-time unlock, and it visibly reverts toward barren wilderness if neglected.

04Systems

How each mechanic should feel, and why
04.1Draft

Mana Economy

One shared mana layer under everything: a personal pool, monster mana cores, item upkeep, and the city core with its storage vaults.
04.2Draft

Character Stats

Six primary attributes, the combat stats they feed, resources, and the order in which modifiers apply.
04.3Draft

Combat

Hybrid tab-target and skill-shot casting. Locks can be broken, aimed shots are hit-or-miss, and allies never block your projectiles.
04.4Draft

Progression

A non-combat passive tree, use-based weapon mastery, and crafting driven by Novice and Master stones.
04.5Draft

PvP, Robbery & PK

PvP that grows out of the economy: stolen cargo, sheriffs and warrants, persistent PK points, tracking and outlaw refuges.
04.6Draft

World Progression

Finite resources, independent city economies, contracts, exploration, and technology unlocked by player discoveries.

The game is in early development. This wiki shows the team’s current design notes: most of them are drafts that will change, and the open gaps are marked “Not designed yet”. It is updated as the design moves.
